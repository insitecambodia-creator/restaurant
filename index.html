The concept

Ficus & Salt opened on the Northbridge waterfront around one idea: everything leaves the kitchen touched by the wood fire, from the octopus to the eggplant to the flatbread. The room is small, the wine list leans natural, and the pace is unhurried by design. Dishes arrive as they're ready, meant for sharing across the table, not for one plate per person.
