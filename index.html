The concept

La Ferme de Bassac began in 2011, when Ludovic Moulin — then personal chef to the French Ambassador to Cambodia — set out to fix a gap he kept running into: quality meat. He started raising pigs on his own organic farm 45 minutes outside Phnom Penh, named after the river running through the capital, and built a butcher counter around it. Every cut, terrine and saucisson served across our bistro, steakhouse and butcher shops still comes from that same farm, raised to EU standards with no hormones and no preventive antibiotics. What started as one counter on Street 178 is now three addresses across Phnom Penh and Siem Reap, run on the same farm-to-counter conviction.
